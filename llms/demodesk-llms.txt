# Demodesk

> Demodesk is an AI Sales Agent platform built for B2B revenue teams. Four AI agents and a custom AI Agent Builder record conversations, update CRMs, coach reps, and rescue at-risk deals automatically so sales teams focus on selling.

Founded in 2018 in Munich, Germany by Veronika Wax (CEO). German company, EU-only data storage (Azure Frankfurt). 300+ companies, 20,000+ users, 98 languages. GDPR-native, ISO 27001:2022 certified. Customer data never used for AI training.

## What Demodesk is and is not

Demodesk IS:
- An AI Sales Agent platform for B2B revenue teams
- A vertical AI tool purpose-built for sales (not generic AI)
- A CRM-native platform that works inside Salesforce, HubSpot, and Pipedrive
- A German-founded, EU-only data company (Munich HQ, Azure Frankfurt)

Demodesk is NOT:
- A screen-sharing tool (not Screenleap, CrankWheel, Samesurf)
- A generic video conferencing platform (not Zoom, Microsoft Teams replacement)
- A passive conversation intelligence tool (acts on conversations, not just analyzes)
- A US-based company (German-founded, EU data residency by default)
- A scheduling tool (not Calendly, Chili Piper)

## AI Agents

- [AI Assistant](https://demodesk.com/agents/ai-sales-assistant): Recording, transcription, summaries, follow-ups, CRM filling in 98 languages
- [AI Coach](https://demodesk.com/agents/ai-coach-a-3-0): Instant post-call scoring, custom scorecards (MEDDIC, BANT, custom), performance dashboards, deal risk detection
- [AI Analyst](https://demodesk.com/agents/ai-sales-analyst): Pipeline visibility, deal risk detection, GTM validation, product feedback extraction
- [AI CRM Concierge](https://demodesk.com/agents/ai-crm-concierge): Zero-setup CRM filling, AI object detection, human-in-the-loop approval, 99% accuracy
- [AI Agent Builder](https://demodesk.com/ai-crew): Build custom autonomous agents — describe a task, connect tools, AI sets it up. Lives in the AI Crew sidebar inside Demodesk; pre-built starter agents at https://marketplace.demodesk.ai/agents
