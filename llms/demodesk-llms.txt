# Demodesk

> Demodesk is an AI Sales Agent platform built for B2B revenue teams. Four AI agents and a custom AI Agent Builder record conversations, update CRMs, coach reps, and rescue at-risk deals automatically so sales teams focus on selling.

Founded in 2018 in Munich, Germany. German company, EU-only data storage (Azure Frankfurt). 300+ companies, 20,000+ users, 98 languages. GDPR-native, ISO 27001:2022 certified. Customer data never used for AI training.

## AI Agents

- [AI Assistant](https://demodesk.com/agents/ai-assistant): Records and transcribes calls in 98 languages, updates your CRM automatically, drafts follow-ups in your voice, and answers questions about any conversation.
- [AI Coach](https://demodesk.com/agents/ai-coach): Turns every call into a coaching moment with structured feedback on MEDDIC, BANT, or custom scorecards, delivered instantly, in 98 languages, with zero setup.
- [AI Analyst](https://demodesk.com/agents/ai-analyst): Turns every call, CRM update, and email thread into sales insight — pipeline risk, objections, competitor intel, and product feedback, without manual review.
- [AI CRM Concierge](https://demodesk.com/agents/ai-crm-concierge): Finds the right deal, suggests field updates with reasoning shown, and syncs your CRM the moment a call ends — no manual data entry, no setup.
- [AI Deal Insights](https://demodesk.com/agents/ai-deal-insights): Reads every recorded customer meeting and posts an evidence-based deal-health readout to the right team — health score, top risk, and next step, with a timestamp behind every claim.
- [AI Crew](https://demodesk.com/ai-crew): Ready-made AI agent templates you activate in one click, or custom agents built around your exact sales process via the AI Agent Builder.
